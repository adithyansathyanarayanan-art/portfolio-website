Futility

Year — 2025 Tools — Procreate

Overview

This idea sparked from Lovecraftian horror, or basically fear of the unknown. The idea of visiting a new continent or an unknown place has always been recorded in travelogues and journals and have had this veil of uncertainty and fear. I wanted to expand on that concept.

The approach

Diary entries are from what I've noticed a very effective way of introducing a world without being obvious. It is a way people would understand the real world so it felt apt to use that method to introduce a fictional one.

With the painting, i wanted to showcase a looming threat or a figurative showcase of death. I found it in the Swedish movie called "The Seventh Seal", in which death is a robed figure only with its face exposed, showing a pale almost ghostly face, with dark sunken eyes.
